      <h2>Method</h2>
      <ul class="note" style="padding-left:18px">
        <li><b>Loads.</b> Four weight/CG conditions; wing/canard lift split from force and moment balance; Schrenk spanwise lift with panel inertia relief; aileron, elevator and rudder effects from thin-airfoil flap theory.</li>
        <li><b>Cases.</b> Symmetric manoeuvre and gust corners (FAR 23-style), rolling pull-outs, abrupt pitch-up with the canard at CLmax, rudder and lateral gusts, level landing, side load, braked roll, and a 9 g forward emergency landing.</li>
        <li><b>Failure modes.</b> Cap strength and skin wrinkling (strain-limited, with hot-wet and impact knock-downs), web shear and web wrinkling, skin torsion, ply-drop interlaminar shear, core flatwise tension, bonded overlaps, bolt bearing at fittings, insert pull-out, fuselage bending and panel shear buckling, gear bending and interlaminar shear, prop-clearance stiffness, divergence, aileron reversal and canard twist.</li>
        <li><b>Sizing.</b> Spar caps ply by ply at 61 stations; webs, hard-point doublers, CS spar caps and the gear bow scaled to the target margin.</li>
      </ul>
      <details><summary>Sources</summary><ul>
        <li>Long-EZ gross weight and cockpit layout: Long-EZ Owners Manual (POH).</li>
        <li>Bulkhead stations: builder logs and CanardZone forum.</li>
        <li>Unidirectional glass/epoxy properties: Samborsky, Mandell &amp; Agastra, Montana State University.</li>
        <li>Load-factor categories and load-case rules: 14 CFR Part 23 (utility category).</li>
        <li>Foam, bearing, ILSS and environmental factors: typical published values (assumed for this demonstration).</li>
      </ul></details>
